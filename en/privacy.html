Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

In short

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone.

What is stored

Only on your phone: your recipes and their photos, your meal plan, your shopping list, your profile (first name, date of birth, height, sex, activity level, goal), your weigh-ins, the water you drink, your streak, your saved foods, your settings and your Groq key. The publisher has no access to them.

Once a week, Goulu writes a copy of that data (without your Groq key) to a file in the Goulu folder on your phone, and keeps only the last three. That file goes nowhere unless you share it yourself.

What leaves your phone

  • Goulu (AI): when you use an AI feature (importing a recipe from text or a photo, suggesting a recipe or options, sorting your recipes, filling your week, sorting items), the text or photo involved (recipe, ingredients, recipe or item names) is sent with your key to Groq, Inc. (United States) to generate the answer. Nothing is sent if you do not use these features. That data is handled under Groq's privacy policy.
  • Brands (Open Food Facts): when you search for a brand or scan a barcode, and when the recipe calculator meets an ingredient the Ciqual table does not know (for example "sachet de béchamel Maggi"), the name of that ingredient or the barcode is sent to Open Food Facts (a French non-profit) to find the product. Nothing else is sent; as with any website, they see your IP address.
  • Barcode scanning: it goes through the Google Play services scanner. The picture stays on your phone, but Google receives usage statistics for that scanner, under its own rules.
  • Updates: the app asks GitHub whether a new version exists, and downloads it from GitHub when you install it. As with any website, GitHub then sees your IP address.
  • Feedback: if you choose "Send feedback", your mail app sends your message to lxm6.pro@gmail.com. Your email address and message are only used to reply and to improve Goulu.
  • Sharing: when you share your shopping list or a backup, you choose the app and the recipient.

What Goulu does not do

No ads, no analytics or audience measurement, no selling or sharing of data for commercial purposes.

Android permissions

  • Notifications: for the weigh-in, evening and water reminders you turn on yourself, and the cooking timers you start.
  • Exact alarms: so those reminders and timers arrive on time.
  • Install apps: to install the updates you start.
  • Camera and photos: only if you add a photo to a recipe or import a recipe from a photo.

Backups

The exported file contains all your data except your Groq key. Keep it somewhere safe: anyone with access to it can read your content.

Your rights

Since your data is on your phone, you control it: you can view it, export it (Settings > Data) or erase everything (Settings > Erase everything). For a feedback email, you can ask to access, correct or delete it by writing to lxm6.pro@gmail.com. You can also contact your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

Retention

Your data stays on your phone until you erase it or uninstall Goulu. Feedback emails are deleted on request, and at the latest three years after the last exchange.

Changes

This policy may change; the update date then changes.

Contact

For any question about your data: lxm6.pro@gmail.com.
